# Concept Seed: Salubriosity Cycling Kit — Hughesdale Flyers
We want to design a next-generation cycling kit for the Hughesdale Flyers club
that expresses "salubriosity" — the quality of promoting health and wellbeing —
through visual design, materials, and performance symbolism.
Key tensions to explore:
- How do you make "healthy/wholesome" feel exciting and fast, not clinical?
- What does wellbeing look like at speed?
- How does this connect to Hughesdale as a place and community?
- Performance kit vs. identity/values expression — can they be unified?
