# Holding-Pattern Checklist

## Practical Actions While You Decide Nothing

*Things to do when you're not ready to make real decisions*

---

## How to Use This Checklist

This is a list of **small, reversible, low-stakes actions** you can take while you figure out what you actually want to do.

**None of these are decisions.** They're housekeeping. They keep things tidy while you think.

Do as many or as few as you want. Order doesn't matter.

---

## The Essentials (Do These)

### 1. Park the Money Safely

**Action:** Move funds to a high-interest savings account with no lock-in period.

| What to Look For | Why |
|------------------|-----|
| No fixed term / no lock-in | You can access it anytime |
| Reasonable interest rate | Better than 0%, but don't obsess |
| Established bank | Covered by government guarantee up to $250k |

**Australian options (as examples, not endorsements):**
- Most major banks have online savings accounts with bonus interest
- Compare at canstar.com.au or mozo.com.au

**Time required:** 15-30 minutes to open account; 1-2 days to transfer funds

**Decision level:** None. This is just storage.

☐ Done

---

### 2. Check Government Guarantee Coverage

**Action:** If you have more than $250,000 in a single institution, consider spreading across multiple banks.

**Why:** Australian government guarantees deposits up to $250k per person per institution. Above that, you're unprotected if the bank fails (unlikely, but possible).

**What to do:**
- If under $250k: Nothing needed
- If over $250k: Open accounts at 2+ different banks and spread funds

**Time required:** 30 minutes if needed

**Decision level:** Administrative. Not a real decision.

☐ Done / ☐ Not applicable

## Super Basics (15 Minutes Total)

### 3. Log Into Your Super

**Action:** Just look at it. No changes required.

**What to check:**
- [ ] Which fund are you in?
- [ ] What are the annual fees? (Under 1% is generally fine)
- [ ] What investment option are you in? (Usually named like "Balanced," "Growth," "High Growth")
- [ ] Who is your nominated beneficiary?

**Time required:** 10 minutes

☐ Done

---

### 4. Consolidate Super (Optional)

**Action:** If you have multiple super accounts from different jobs, consider combining them.

**Why:** Multiple accounts = multiple fees. Consolidating saves money.

**How:**
- Use the ATO's myGov portal (my.gov.au → ATO → Super)
- Click "Manage" → "Transfer super"
- Follow prompts

**Caution:** Check for:
- Any insurance attached to old accounts (you might lose it)
- Exit fees on older accounts

**Time required:** 15-20 minutes

**Decision level:** Fairly low stakes. But fine to skip if unsure.

☐ Done / ☐ Skipped for now

---

### 5. Check Super Beneficiary

**Action:** Make sure your super goes to who you want if something happens to you.

**Types:**
- **Binding nomination:** Legally enforced. Must be valid dependants.
- **Non-binding nomination:** Trustee decides, but considers your preference.

**What to do:**
- Log into super fund
- Find "Beneficiaries" section
- Update if needed

**Time required:** 5 minutes

☐ Done

## Financial Hygiene (Optional But Useful)

### 6. Review Your Bank Accounts

**Action:** Check if your current accounts are costing you money or earning nothing.

| Check | Action if No |
|-------|--------------|
| Is your everyday account fee-free? | Consider switching |
| Is your savings account earning interest? | Consider moving savings |
| Do you have accounts you never use? | Consider closing |

**Time required:** 15 minutes

☐ Done / ☐ Skipped

---

### 7. Check Your Spending Patterns

**Action:** Look at last 3 months of transactions. No judgment—just data.

**What to notice:**
- [ ] Any subscriptions you forgot about?
- [ ] Any recurring charges you don't recognise?
- [ ] Roughly what % goes to rent/housing, food, transport, entertainment?

**You don't need to change anything.** Just look.

**Time required:** 15-20 minutes

☐ Done / ☐ Skipped

---

### 8. Know Your Tax Situation

**Action:** Understand the basics of how this money affects your tax.

**Key points (Australia):**
- Gifts and inheritances are generally **not taxed** when received
- Interest earned on the money **is** taxable income
- If you invest and make capital gains, those are taxable

**What to do:**
- Note how much interest you earn this financial year
- If significant (>$1,000), may affect your tax return
- Consider seeing a tax accountant at EOFY if unsure

**Decision level:** Awareness only. No decisions needed now.

☐ Understood

## Protection Basics (One-Time Setup)

### 9. Do You Have a Will?

**Action:** Check if you have a valid will.

**Reality:** At 20-23, most people don't have one. That's normal. But if you have significant assets, it becomes more relevant.

**Options:**
- DIY will kit (cheap but risky if complex)
- Online will service (reasonably priced)
- Lawyer (most thorough, costs $300-800+)

**Decision level:** Optional. But worth knowing where you stand.

☐ Have a will / ☐ Don't have one / ☐ Will look into it

---

### 10. Understand Your Insurance

**Action:** Know what insurance you already have.

**You probably have:**
- **Super insurance:** Most super funds include life insurance and income protection. Check your statements.
- **Medicare:** Covers basic health.
- **Maybe:** Health insurance if family policy includes you.

**What to check:**
- What cover does your super provide?
- Are you still on family health insurance?

**No action needed.** Just awareness.

☐ Done

## Things to NOT Do Right Now

This checklist is about holding steady. These things can wait:

| Not Yet | Why |
|---------|-----|
| ☐ Don't invest (unless you want to) | No rush. Cash is fine for now. |
| ☐ Don't buy property | Major decision. Deserves proper consideration. |
| ☐ Don't lend to friends/family | Hard to undo. Relationship-changing. |
| ☐ Don't hire a financial adviser yet | Wait until you know what questions you have. |
| ☐ Don't tell everyone | Creates pressure. Keep options open. |
| ☐ Don't make any major irreversible commitments | The point of a holding pattern is optionality. |

## The "If You Do Nothing Else" Version

If this whole checklist feels like too much, just do this:

1. **Open a high-interest savings account** (any reputable bank)
2. **Put the money there**
3. **Stop**

That's a valid holding pattern. Everything else can wait.

---

## Check-In Prompts

Set a reminder for 3-6 months. Ask yourself:

- [ ] Is the money still safe?
- [ ] Have I learned anything about what I want?
- [ ] Am I closer to being ready, or still waiting?
- [ ] Am I waiting well, or avoiding?

**If waiting well:** Continue.
**If avoiding:** Consider talking to someone (adviser, therapist, trusted person).
