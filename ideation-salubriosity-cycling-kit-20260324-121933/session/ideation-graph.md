## Connections

- **"Careful observation as love"**: Both the naturalist tradition and the word "salubriosity" share a root impulse — looking closely at living things in service of their flourishing. This connection is what locks the aesthetic to the concept.
- **Palette unifies place and tradition**: The Australian morning-light palette is simultaneously the colour of this specific suburb and the colour of Australian natural history illustration. The same palette does two jobs.
- **Motion as evidence, not noise**: The motion-evidence concept reframes speed as the revelatory condition for the design, not an obstacle to it. This pattern could extend beyond the kit to any communication about the club.
- **Victorian anachronism as differentiator**: The word "salubriosity" signals that this club is not speaking the language of modern wellness brands. The naturalist aesthetic doubles down on that signal. Their identity is grounded in an older, more considered tradition.
