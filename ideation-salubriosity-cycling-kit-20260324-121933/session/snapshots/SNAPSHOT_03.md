## Emerging Patterns

The three interesting ideas are not independent — they are aspects of one unified concept. Thread A (the naturalist field guide) is the identity and visual language. Thread B (motion-evidence) is the performance expression. Thread C (the morning palette) is the place and colour language. Together they form a complete answer to all four tensions in the brief.

The session's key discovery: the word "salubriosity" is not a brand proposition to be illustrated. It is an aesthetic tradition to be inhabited. The naturalist movement was about salubriosity in the original sense — careful attention to living things as a form of health promotion. The club chose a word that already contained their answer.
